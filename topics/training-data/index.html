Interval training, exact pitch data and reproducibility

Practice rational intervals and access versioned mathematical data with academic citation.

The training workspace offers theory questions and ear training. The exact pitch table provides a reference for each of the 18 ascending positions, including normalized string length, frequency ratio and cents.

Reproducibility

Frequency ratios are reciprocals of string-length fractions; the octave is 2/1. Cents are computed from 1200 log₂(r) and rounded for display. Tests check reduced fractions, ascending order, octave closure and the adjacent limma/comma sequence.
