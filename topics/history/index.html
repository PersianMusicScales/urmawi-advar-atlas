Ṣafī al-Dīn al-Urmawī and Kitāb al-Adwār

Historical context, manuscript sources and editorial interpretation of the Book of Cycles.

Ṣafī al-Dīn al-Urmawī (also written Safi al-Din al-Urmawi or Safiyyüddin Urmevî) is associated with the Systematist tradition of medieval music theory. His Kitāb al-Adwār (Book of Cycles, also called Kitab al-Adwar or Kitâbü’l-Edvâr) provides the historical framework for this atlas.

Reading the manuscript

The edition follows British Library Or 136, digitized by the Qatar Digital Library. Section Two (ff. 3v–5r) concerns fret divisions, Section Three (ff. 5r–8r) interval ratios, Section Six (ff. 11r–18r) modal combinations, and Section Eight (ff. 18v–19v) lute tuning and derivation.

Historical evidence versus modern presentation

The interactive geometry translates proportional relationships into visual operations. It does not reproduce the manuscript word for word. The digital audio and instrument drawing are modern learning aids, not documentary evidence of a particular historical performance.
