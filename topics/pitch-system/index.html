The 17-tone pitch lattice and exact interval ratios

Unequal limmas and Pythagorean commas, rational pitch relationships and cent values.

The atlas represents 17 unequal steps in an octave using 18 points including the octave repetition. This is a 3-limit mathematical model, not 17-tone equal temperament.

Twelve limmas and five commas multiply to exactly 2/1: (256/243)¹² × (531441/524288)⁵ = 2. A tone at frequency ratio r has interval size 1200 log₂(r) cents. These numerical relations are mathematical, not claims about a uniform historical tuning practice.
