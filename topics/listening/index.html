Listening to historical intervals and microtonal pitch

Frequency calculations and comparative listening using modern pure tones and plucked-string models.

With a reference frequency f₀, a pitch at ratio r is generated at frequency f₀ × r. For an open pitch of 220 Hz, the fourth (4/3) is approximately 293.333 Hz, the fifth (3/2) is 330 Hz, and the octave (2/1) is 440 Hz.

Compare by listening

Choose two pitches and listen successively or simultaneously in the Sound Atlas. A pure oscillator provides a simple frequency reference; the plucked-string model offers an alternative modern synthetic timbre. Changing the base frequency changes absolute frequency, not the theoretical interval ratio or its cent value.

These listening models are instructional. They do not reconstruct an original medieval instrument or establish a unique historical intonation.
