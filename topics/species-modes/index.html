Tetrachord species and historical modal categories

How interval categories and fourth-based species relate to the parent pitch lattice.

The Atlas distinguishes exact ratio relationships from categorical interval descriptions. The common tone–tone–limma fourth is an exact identity: (9/8) × (9/8) × (256/243) = 4/3.

From species to adwār

The Systematist literature discusses categories such as ṭanīnī (T), mujannab (J) and baqiyyah (B). The atlas illustrates species and combinations of lower tetrachords and upper pentachords. Categories should not automatically be assigned fixed modern cent values across all theoretical and performance contexts.

For a modern analytical discussion of Iṣfahān, see Poorhaydari (2025), Maqām Iṣfahān, §§1.3–1.5. The application separates the numerical model from interpretive modal descriptions.
