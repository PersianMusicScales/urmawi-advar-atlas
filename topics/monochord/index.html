Monochord geometry and mathematical construction

How exact string divisions produce frequencies, fourths, fifths and octaves.

The geometric model normalizes the full sounding string to length 1. Assuming unchanged tension and linear mass density, frequency is inversely proportional to sounding length. If the sounding length is L, the frequency ratio to the open string is 1/L.

Worked construction

  1. Keep the full string: length 1/1, frequency ratio 1/1.
  2. Divide the string into two and retain one part: length 1/2, octave 2/1.
  3. Retain two of three equal parts: length 2/3, fifth 3/2.
  4. Retain three of four equal parts: length 3/4, fourth 4/3.
  5. Apply the three-quarter length relation again: (3/4)² = 9/16, frequency 16/9.
