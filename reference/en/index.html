Al-Urmawī Advar Atlas: 17-tone system and Kitāb al-Adwār

Ṣafī al-Dīn al-Urmawī (Safi al-Din al-Urmawi) describes a system of fret divisions and intervals in Kitāb al-Adwār (Book of Cycles). This reference accompanies the interactive atlas and presents its exact 3-limit mathematical model.

Pitch, string length and intervals

For a normalized sounding string length L, the frequency ratio is 1/L and the interval in cents is 1200 log₂(1/L). The octave contains 17 unequal steps (18 pitch points including the repeated octave): twelve limmas (256/243, about 90.225 cents) and five Pythagorean commas (531441/524288, about 23.460 cents). This is not 17-tone equal temperament.

Sources and editorial limits

The Atlas locates fret divisions in Kitāb al-Adwār, Section Two, ff. 3v–5r; interval ratios in Section Three, ff. 5r–8r; modal combinations in Section Six, ff. 11r–18r; and lute tuning in Section Eight, ff. 18v–19v. Its geometric walkthrough, modern prose and synthesized sound are pedagogical interpretations, not a diplomatic transcription or a unique reconstruction of medieval performance practice. Specialist review of translated historical terms remains desirable.
